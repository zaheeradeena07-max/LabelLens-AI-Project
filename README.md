# 🥗 LabelLens AI — Smart Food Label Analyzer

**Understand what's inside your food. Make smarter food choices.**

LabelLens AI is an AI-powered food-label analysis website designed to help users understand packaged food products by analysing their ingredient lists and nutritional information. It simplifies confusing food labels by identifying ingredients of concern, highlighting nutritional values, and providing easy-to-understand insights. With a focus on personalized nutrition, allergy awareness, and healthier food choices, LabelLens AI helps users make more informed decisions about the products they consume.

## 🌐 Live Website

**Try LabelLens AI:** https://label-lens-ai-project.vercel.app/

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Packaged food labels often contain complex ingredient names, additives, and nutritional information that consumers may find difficult to understand. People with food allergies, dietary restrictions, or specific health goals may struggle to determine whether a product suits their needs.

LabelLens AI addresses this challenge by simplifying food-label information and helping users identify relevant ingredients and nutritional concerns.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 AI-Powered Food Label Analysis
- Upload a food-label image for analysis.
- Extract and interpret available ingredient and nutritional information.
- Present complex label information in simple language.

### 🧪 Ingredient and Nutrition Insights
- Identify ingredients that may require attention.
- Highlight nutritional values such as sugar, fat, protein, and sodium, when available.
- Explain nutritional information in a user-friendly format.

### 🛡️ Allergy-Aware Food Choices
- Identify ingredients associated with user-reported allergies.
- Highlight potential allergen concerns when detectable from the available label information.
- Help users review ingredient information before choosing a product.

### 📊 Personalized Nutrition Guidance
- Support food recommendations based on dietary preferences and personal goals.
- Help users understand how products may fit their nutritional needs.
- Provide general guidance for making informed food choices.

### 🔄 Smart Food Alternatives
- Suggest alternative food items based on dietary preferences and nutritional goals.
- Explain relevant differences between the original item and a suggested alternative when information is available.

### ⚖️ Health and Wellness Support
- Support healthier eating and general weight-management goals.
- Provide food-related guidance to encourage informed dietary choices.
